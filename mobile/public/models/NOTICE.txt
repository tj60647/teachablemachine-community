Source: https://storage.googleapis.com/tfjs-models/tfjs/mobilenet_v1_0.25_224/model.json

This application freezes the convolutional layers and trains a small classification head locally.
The bundled model has been modified to end at conv_pw_13_relu with global average
pooling. scripts/prepare-model.mjs reproduces this modification from the source.
